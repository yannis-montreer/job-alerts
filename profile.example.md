# Search profile

Copy this file to `profile.md` and replace the examples. `profile.md` is git-ignored.
It is sent as-is to Claude at each run to sort the ads: write it in plain language (any
language), be concrete, and only state facts that are in your CV. The clearer your
criteria, the better the sorting.

## Profile
- Years of experience, level (junior / experienced / senior), education.
- Where your experience comes from (countries, company types).
- Where you live.

## Experience
- Company (city), job title, dates: what you did, with numbers if you have them.
- ...

## Target roles
- Example: Business Developer, SDR / BDR, Account Executive, B2B sales.
- Roles that are acceptable only under conditions (say which).

## Sectors
- Main sectors, then secondary ones.

## Strengths
- What makes you a good fit (skills, tools, results).

## Languages
- Your languages and levels.
- How to treat ads that require Norwegian. Example: "An ad requiring Norwegian stays relevant
  if the rest fits: flag it and I decide", or "Norwegian required = not relevant".

## Location
- Target area, remote work, commuting limits.

## Rejection criteria
- Roles, levels or conditions to reject (e.g. team management, commission only, call center).
- Requirements to flag rather than reject (e.g. a master's degree).

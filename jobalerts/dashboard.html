You & search

Who the ads are for, the language, the area, and your profile.

Basics

Area

NAV ads are kept when their municipality is checked here, or their county is in the county list.

Profile

Sent as-is to Claude at each run. Describe experience, target roles, sectors, languages and what to reject. Only facts from your CV.

Telegram

The bot that sends you the ads, with Keep / Reject buttons.

1. Create your bot

  1. In Telegram, open @BotFather and send /newbot.
  2. Choose a name and a username ending in bot.
  3. Copy the token it gives you and paste it below. Keep it private.

2. Link your chat

  1. Open your bot in Telegram and send it any message (e.g. "hello").
  2. Click Detect, then Use this chat next to your name.

3. Test

Don't run another program with the same bot token: it would read your clicks before this tool does.

AI

The AI that reads each ad and sorts it against your profile, once a day.

Which AI?

Gemini

  1. Open Google AI Studio and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Create an API key and paste it below. Keep it private.

A Gemini app subscription does not include an API key, but the free tier of the API is enough for one call a day. On the free tier, Google's terms allow it to use what is sent (your profile and the day's ads) to improve its products.

Finn alerts (optional)

Finn.no ads arrive through your own Finn alert emails, read from Gmail.

1. Create the alert on Finn

  1. On finn.no, search jobs with your area and categories, then save the search as an alert (Søkeagent) sent by email.
  2. Use the Gmail address below to receive it.

The tool reads only those emails and never downloads finn.no pages: Finn's terms forbid automated access. Finn ads therefore show title, employer and place only; open the link to read them.

2. Connect Gmail

  1. Turn on 2-step verification on your Google account.
  2. Create an app password and paste it below (not your normal password).
